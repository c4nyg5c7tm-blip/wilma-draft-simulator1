Reach analysis

2025 reach calibration uses ESPN's Sept. 2, 2025 PPR Top 200 as the reference rank. Positive reach means a manager drafted earlier than the reference rank. This is layered on top of the simulator's existing 2020–2025 round/position history.

Opponent realism engine

Phase 1 models each owner as an individual drafter. It combines historical position timing, 2025 reach behavior, roster state, NFL-team/player affinity, sample size and prediction confidence instead of treating every opponent as the same AI.

Draft pressure & steal map

Phase 2 estimates who is most likely to take a player before your next turn. It also detects position pressure, likely runs, and the opponent most likely to trigger the steal.

Wilma strategy engine

Phase 3 gives you three views: what your historical profile would do, what the model thinks maximizes league success, and a hybrid that corrects your historical habits when the value is better elsewhere.

What the AI is using

Each opponent gets a round-by-round positional tendency from your historical file, then blends that tendency with current tier/rank, roster need, and the selected randomness level.
